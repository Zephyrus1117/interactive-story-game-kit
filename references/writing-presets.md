# Writing Presets

Choose one prose preset before writing nodes. Presets control sentence length, dialogue density, clarity, and acceptable stylization.

## plain-drama

- Use when the story must be highly readable.
- Sentences: short to medium.
- Dialogue: direct, emotionally clear.
- Avoid: dense metaphor, delayed premise, symbolic endings.

## clear-suspense

- Use for mystery, thriller, horror, investigation.
- Put clue before interpretation.
- Each scene should answer one question and open one sharper question.
- Avoid: clues that are only vibes; all answers held until the finale.

## campus-contemporary

- Use for school, youth, slice-of-life, light romance.
- Natural speech, texts, schedules, small social stakes that become larger.
- Avoid: overly literary narration for everyday moments.

## romance-visual

- Use for route-based emotional stories.
- Let choices reveal values and boundaries, not only affection points.
- Avoid: opaque love interests, instant route endings, generic confession scenes.

## palace-formal

- Use for court, ancient, political, family-clan drama.
- Style can be formal, but every sentence must remain easy to parse.
- Avoid: too many titles before the player knows factions.

## wuxia-road

- Use for martial-world travel, sect conflict, revenge, justice.
- Keep period flavor in verbs, objects, rituals, and etiquette.
- Avoid: archaic filler that hides the action.

## cyber-terminal

- Use for cyberpunk, hacking, surveillance, AI systems.
- Use concise operational language with occasional emotional breaks.
- Avoid: pages of jargon without a human objective.

## horror-house

- Use for haunted location, survival, dread.
- Fear should come from specific threats, rules, and costs.
- Avoid: pure darkness, pure whispers, pure hallucination.

## fairy-tale-dark

- Use for fable, magic, allegory.
- Keep symbolic objects concrete and repeated with changing meaning.
- Avoid: morals stated too early or endings that dodge causality.

## absurd-comedy

- Use for workplace satire, surreal comedy, strange quests.
- The scene logic can be absurd; character goals must still be clear.
- Avoid: random jokes that do not affect choice or consequence.

## Script-Level Clarity Pass

After drafting, revise every node once:

- Replace vague emotion with visible action where possible.
- Move necessary facts earlier.
- Shorten any sentence that contains three or more nested clauses.
- Make choice labels concrete.
- Cut metaphors that repeat the same mood.
